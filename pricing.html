FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need trading experience to join?
Not at all! Our Starter plan is designed specifically for beginners. We'll teach you everything from the basics of chart reading to building your first strategy. Many of our most successful students started with zero experience.
What markets do you cover?
We primarily focus on Forex pairs, but our strategies and analysis also cover gold (XAU/USD), major indices, and select cryptocurrencies. Our methodology works across all liquid markets.
How are trading signals delivered?
Signals are delivered via our private Discord channel and Telegram group in real time. Each signal includes the entry price, stop loss, and take profit levels with a clear risk-reward breakdown and trade rationale.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes! There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime from your account dashboard. If you cancel within the first 7 days, you'll receive a full refund as part of our money-back guarantee.
What makes The Blue Candle different?
We focus on education first, not just signals. Our approach combines technical analysis with trading psychology and risk management. We don't promise overnight riches — we teach you how to build a sustainable, long-term trading career.
